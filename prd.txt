# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# InvestWall

### AI-Driven Detection of Synthetic Media & Phishing Attacks for Retail Investors

Version: 1.0

Platform: Android

---

# 1. Overview

InvestWall is an Android application designed to protect retail investors from AI-generated fraud, phishing attacks, deepfakes, impersonation attempts, and manipulated financial content.

The application analyzes content received through SMS, email, WhatsApp, social media, documents, images, videos, and audio before the user acts on it.

Unlike traditional spam filters, InvestWall performs multimodal AI analysis and produces an explainable trust score by combining evidence from specialized AI detection models.

The application is designed around the problem statement of detecting synthetic media while also verifying authentic financial communications.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Generative AI has drastically reduced the effort required to create convincing scams.

Examples include:

* AI-generated phishing emails
* Deepfake CEO videos
* Voice cloning of financial advisors
* Fake SEBI/NSE announcements
* AI-written investment scams
* Manipulated market news
* Pump-and-dump campaigns on social media

Retail investors often lack the expertise to determine whether content is authentic.

InvestWall serves as a personal AI-powered trust layer between incoming content and the user's decision.

---

# 3. Objectives

The application should:

* Detect AI-generated images
* Detect AI-generated videos
* Detect AI-generated voice/audio
* Detect AI-generated text
* Detect phishing attacks
* Detect impersonation attempts
* Verify authenticity of financial communications
* Explain why content was flagged
* Provide an easy-to-understand Trust Score
